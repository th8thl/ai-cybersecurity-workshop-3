Exercise 2

Semantic search finds related meaning

Compare “I forgot my credentials” using Keyword and Semantic search. Explain why similarity does not mean truth, permission or confidence.

Keyword

Matches exact tokens. A document saying “account recovery” may not contain “forgot credentials.”

Semantic

Compares learned representations. Inspect the actual ranking; proximity is approximate and can retrieve irrelevant or malicious material.

Lower cosine distance = more similar. It is not a probability that the answer is correct.
